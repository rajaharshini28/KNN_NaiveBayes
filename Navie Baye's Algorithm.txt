Navie Baye's Algorithm


 we use it to check the probability of the particular event
 
Independent events:Rolling a dice
(Output cannot be predictable)

Dependent Event: One is dependent on other event

Baye's Theorem:P(A/B)=(P(A)*P(B/A))/P(B)
P(A/B):Probability of A given B has occurred.
P(B/A):Probability of B given A has occurred.

P(Y/x1,x2,x3):(P(Y)*P((x1,x2,x3)/Y))/P(x1,x2,x3)

P(Yes/x1,x2,x3):P(Yes)*(P(x1,x2,x3)/P(Yes))/P(No)*(P(x1,x2,x3)/P(No))
P(No/x1,x2,x3):P(No)*(P(x1,x2,x3)/P(No))
